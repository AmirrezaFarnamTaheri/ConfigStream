Our Mission

To provide free, uncensored, and secure internet access to everyone, everywhere. We believe information is a human right.

Zero Budget, Maximum Impact

We operate entirely on free infrastructure (GitHub Actions/Pages). This guarantees sustainability—we can't run out of funds because we don't spend any.

Automated Intelligence

A hybrid Python/Go engine aggregates, deduplicates, tests, and "washes" thousands of proxies on a recurring schedule — fully automated.

Radical Transparency

Open source from day one. Every line of code, every metric, and every configuration is public and auditable by the community.
